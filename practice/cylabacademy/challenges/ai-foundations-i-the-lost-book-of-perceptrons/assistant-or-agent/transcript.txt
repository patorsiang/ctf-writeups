END OF ASSISTANT OR AGENT?

Key takeaways:
1. AI assistants respond with outputs; they do not act unless a human does.
2. AI agents can plan and execute actions with tools and memory.
3. Agent actions create real side effects, so scope and permissions matter.
4. Guardrails (budgets, allowlists, approval gates) make autonomy safer.
